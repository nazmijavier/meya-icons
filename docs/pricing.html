Questions before you buy

Still deciding? Write to hello@meyalab.com.

What counts as a seat?

One seat for each person who uses the Pro icons. Each teammate accepts Polar's invitation email, then logs in here with their own email.

How is a team price worked out?

Each seat has its own yearly rate: the first seat is $60, seats 2 to 5 are $22 each, and every seat from the 6th is $10. Three seats cost $60 + $22 + $22 = $104 a year.Each seat has its own yearly rate: the first seat is Rp790.000, seats 2 to 5 are Rp290.000 each, and every seat from the 6th is Rp130.000. Three seats cost Rp790.000 + Rp290.000 + Rp290.000 = Rp1.370.000 a year.

How does the launch offer work?

Every Get Pro button on this page applies the launch discount for you, so there's no code to type: 50% off your first year. From your second year, Pro renews at the regular price shown on the card.

How do I get the Pro icons after I buy?

Log in on this site with the email you bought with. Polar, our payment provider, emails you a 6-character sign-in code, or you can use your license key. Then open any Pro icon and copy it as SVG or React.

Can I use the icons in client work?

Yes. Pro icons can be used in personal and client work. Outline and Duotone are MIT licensed for personal and commercial work; credit is appreciated but not required.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from the Polar customer portal linked in your receipt email. Pro stays active until the end of the year you paid for; after that it doesn't renew and copying Pro icons stops.

Are Pro icons in the npm package or the Figma plugin?

No. The npm package and the free Figma plugin carry Outline and Duotone. Pro icons are copied from this site as SVG or React.

Why are prices in US dollars?Why are prices in Rupiah?

Prices show in one currency, chosen by where you are: Rupiah in Indonesia, US dollars everywhere else.
