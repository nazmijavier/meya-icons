Frequently asked questions

Still have questions? Write to hello@meyalab.com.

What's included in Pro?

Sharp, Filled and Glass: 864 icons on top of the free Outline and Duotone sets, plus new icons as they're released.

How does per-seat pricing work?

Each person who uses the Pro icons needs a seat. Pick the number of seats at checkout; each seat is $5 a month.

Can I use the icons in commercial projects?

Yes. Outline and Duotone are MIT licensed, and Pro icons can be used in personal and client work.

How do I get the Pro icons?

Right after checkout you get a download link by email with every Pro style as SVG files.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription from the customer portal link in your receipt email. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Is there a Figma plugin?

Yes. Meya Icons for Figma is free in the Figma Community and places icons straight onto your canvas, with every stroke editable.
